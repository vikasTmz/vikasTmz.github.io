1. Goal: Generative model for watertight B-reps
The goal of this project is to build a generative model for B-Reps. B-Reps (boundary representations) encode parametric surfaces together with precise topological structure.At inference time -- either unconditionally or conditioned on a text-prompt or a point cloud or an image -- the goal is to sample shapes represented as B-Reps that are compatible with various design, animation, and engineering software.
2. Leveraging pre-trained 3D generative models
We witnessed huge process in the last two years in generative models for 3D shapes. These models, conditioned on images or text-prompts, can sample high quality 3D shapes represented as implicit fields or meshes or some custom 3D data structure.
